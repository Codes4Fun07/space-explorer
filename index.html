Upcoming Missions

Dragonfly

Mission: Dragonfly

Target: Titan, Saturn's largest moon

Launch: NET July 2028

Arrival: Late 2034

Type: Rotorcraft

What it will do:

Dragonfly will fly between different locations on Titan, studying its surface, atmosphere, and chemistry. NASA describes it as the first rotorcraft mission to explore another world. Its goal is to investigate Titan's habitability and the chemistry that may help scientists understand the processes that preceded biology on Earth.

Europa Clipper

Mission: Europa Clipper

Target: Europa, moon of Jupiter

Launch: October 14, 2024 — Already launched

Arrival: April 2030

Type: Orbiter

What it will do:

Europa Clipper will study Europa to determine whether the moon has conditions suitable for life. It will make about 49 close flybys of Europa, investigating its surface, ice shell, and subsurface ocean.

COSI

Mission: COSI — Compton Spectrometer and Imager

Target: The Milky Way and beyond

Type: Gamma-ray telescope

What it will do:

COSI will study high-energy phenomena in the universe using gamma rays. It will investigate the creation and destruction of matter and antimatter and study the final stages of stars.
